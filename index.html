        How points work

        A snipe is a photo of someone posted in #sibfam-sniping with them tagged: 1 point per person tagged. A selfie with your sibs scores 1 for each sib tagged plus 1 for the photo, and each sib in it gets 1. Snipes during the general meeting don't count, and two people can't score off each other again within 15 minutes.
